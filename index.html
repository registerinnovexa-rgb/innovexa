Everything you need to know about joining Innovexa Labs.

Innovexa Labs is the premier student tech collective at Yenepoya University, powered by Kalvium. We forge the next generation of builders through structured bootcamps, a rich knowledge atlas, peer networking, and exclusive membership perks — designed to accelerate your journey from student to industry-ready operative.

Click "Become an Operative" to fill out the registration form. Complete your personal details, pay the ₹599 one-time fee via UPI, and submit your payment screenshot. Your application will be reviewed by the admin team within 24-48 hours.

Your Operative ID is a unique INVX-XX membership identifier assigned upon approval. It comes with a downloadable digital ID card that serves as verifiable proof of your Innovexa Labs membership status.

Once approved, you'll receive your Operative ID and gain access to the exclusive WhatsApp and Telegram community groups. You can download your ID card from the Status page using your registered email or UTR number.

The ₹599 membership fee is non-refundable. It covers your full academic year 2025-26 membership including all events, bootcamps, and community access.
