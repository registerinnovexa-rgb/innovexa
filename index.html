Got Questions?

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before you initialize your journey.

What is Innovexa Labs?
Innovexa Labs is the premier student tech collective at Yenepoya University, powered by Kalvium. We forge the next generation of builders through structured bootcamps, a rich knowledge atlas, peer networking, and exclusive membership perks — designed to accelerate your journey from student to industry-ready operative.
How do I join Innovexa Labs?
Joining is simple: register online via the Register page, complete your profile, and pay the one-time annual membership fee of ₹599. Once your application is reviewed and approved by an admin, you officially become an Operative of Innovexa Labs.
What happens after I register?
After submitting your registration, our admin team reviews your application. Once approved, you'll receive your Operative status and your unique INVX-XX membership ID. The exclusive WhatsApp community group link will be revealed on your Status page, granting you full access to the Innovexa Labs ecosystem.
What is the Operative ID Card?
Every approved Operative receives a unique INVX-XX membership ID — a sequential identifier that marks your place in the Innovexa Labs collective. You can view and download your beautifully designed digital ID Card from the Status page, which serves as verifiable proof of your membership.
Is the ₹599 membership fee refundable?
No. The ₹599 annual membership fee is non-refundable. It covers access to all Innovexa Labs resources, events, bootcamps, and community benefits for the entire 2025-26 academic year. Please ensure you are fully committed before completing your registration.
How long is my membership valid?
Your Innovexa Labs membership is valid for the complete 2025-26 academic year. This includes access to all bootcamps, Atlas resources, community channels, and events scheduled within this period. Renewal information for subsequent batches will be announced toward the end of the academic year.
