Quick Questions
How long does approval take?
Applications are typically reviewed within 24–72 hours after submission. You will be notified via email once a decision is made. If you haven't heard back after 3 days, feel free to contact support.
What is a UTR number?
A UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) is the 12–22 digit reference number generated when you complete your payment via UPI, NEFT, or IMPS. It can be found in your payment app under transaction history or the confirmation SMS you received.
I'm approved — what's next?
Congratulations! Download your Operative ID card, then join the WhatsApp and Telegram community groups using the buttons shown on your status result. Watch out for onboarding messages from the Innovexa core team.
